Peshawar Green Spaces Atlas

Peshawar, the "City of Flowers", has Mughal-era gardens such as Shahi Bagh and Wazir Bagh, the university campus greens, and many small neighbourhood parks. This map shows what volunteers have recorded in OpenStreetMap across the city, from Hayatabad to the old walled city.
